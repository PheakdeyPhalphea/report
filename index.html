1.4. Severity Levels

Common Vulnerabilies and Exposure (CVE) is glossary that classifies Vulnerabilies. The gloassary analyzes vulnerabilies and then uses the Common Vulnerabilies Scoring System (CVSS) to evaluate the threat level of vulnerability. A CVE score is often used for prioritizing the security of vulnerabilities.

The CVSS is one of serveral ways to measure the impact of vulnerability and assign a severity along a scale of 0 - 10.The current version of CVSS is 4.0v, which breaks down the scale as follows:

Common Vulnerabilies Scoring System (CVSS)
Severity Base Score Definition
Critical 9.0-10.0 Critical level risk exposure. Critical potential impact to confidentiality,integrity,and/ or availability.It requires immediate action attenion.
High 7.0-8.9 High level risk exposure.High potential impact to confidentiality,integrity,and/ or availability. But It does not requires immediate action attenion.
Medium 4.0-6.9 Medium level risk exposure.Medium potential impact to confidentiality,integrity,and/ or availability.But It requires review action attention in near term.
Low 0.1-3.9 Minimal or minor risk exposure and/or control improvement point. Low potential impact to confidentiality, integrity, and/or availability.To be reviewed and evaluated after other key issues are addressed.
